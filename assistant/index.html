[ 01 ] — One sample conversation, end to end

Five questions. Only one matters.

Here is a full exchange with the assistant on this page. Nothing is staged: ask it these questions yourself and you will get the same thing. The fourth one is the one we care about.

[ system ] · pinned

The three rules it runs on

  1. It answers only from your information.
  2. When it is not sure, it hands over.
  3. It passes every lead to you.

[ visitor ]What exactly do you install on my site?

[ assistant ]

An assistant trained on your own information. It answers your visitors at any hour, in French, German or English, and it never steps outside what you gave it.

[ visitor ]How long before it is live?

[ assistant ]

About a week. We start with a test version built on your public content. You read a full transcript before a single visitor talks to it.

[ visitor ]And if it tells a client nonsense?

[ assistant ]

That is the rule that comes before all the others: no inventing. If the answer is not in your information, it does not manufacture one. It says so, and it hands over.

[ the moment ]

[ visitor ]Can you plug it into our booking software so it takes appointments?

[ assistant ]

I do not know. That is not part of what I know, and I am not going to invent an answer on a technical point. Leave me your email: someone replies the same day, with a real yes or a real no.

This is where everything is decided. An assistant that invents an answer costs you a client’s trust, and you will never know which client. This one stops, says so, and hands you the question: it is not lost, it changes hands.

[ visitor ]Fine, here is my address.

[ assistant ]

Noted. Your question and your contact details go to the owner of the site. You have nothing else to do: they will get back to you.

These five questions are not decoration. Put them to the assistant in the bottom right corner: if it answers something else, the problem is ours, not yours.

[ 02 ] — What it knows, and nothing else

[ visitor ]So what have you actually read?

[ assistant ]

It has not read the internet. It has read your house.

What we give it

Your information, the part you choose

  • Your pages, your services, the way you work.
  • Your hours, your service areas, your terms — what people ask you ten times a week.
  • Your documents, if you want: an FAQ, a leaflet, an overview of your services.
  • Your limits: whatever it is not allowed to say, you tell us, and it does not say it.

What it does not have

And will therefore never do

  • No improvised general knowledge about your trade.
  • No figure, no lead time, no price that you did not give it.
  • No commitment made on your behalf.
  • No cookie, no tracker: messages are there to answer you, and nothing else.

A useful assistant is not the one that knows everything. It is the one whose limits you know exactly.

[ 03 ] — When it is not sure

[ visitor ]And when you are not sure?

[ assistant ]

It does not bluff. It hands over.

01

It says so

No approximate answer, no vague wording to save face. It states that it does not know, and why: it is not in what it was given.

02

It offers a human

It asks the person how to reach them and tells them what happens next. A visitor who hears “I am passing this on” stays. A visitor given a wrong answer leaves.

03

You get the question

The question and the contact details reach you straight away. You reply to someone who is waiting for you, instead of finding an empty form the next day.

That is the only moment the assistant disturbs you. The rest of the time it answers and you see nothing go by.
