An agency’s problem is almost never skill. It is the inquiry that belongs to everyone, and therefore to no one — especially evenings, weekends and during viewings.

With us, every inquiry gets its answer. Every reply and follow-up is copied to your shared inbox, visible to the whole team: whoever picks up the file on Monday sees exactly what was answered.

[ Agencies only ] — built for how an agency works

Not a generic assistant. An agency's working day.

i

Viewings that book themselves

The reply offers viewing times from your calendar as one-click buttons. Your hours, your minimum notice, never a double booking — and you and the client both receive the invitation. Cancelling and moving are handled too.

ii

The rental file, before you call

For rentals, the reply gathers what you always need — move-in date, number of occupants, income or guarantor — without ever asking for documents by email. When the file is complete, you are told: ready to present to the owner.

iii

Sellers come straight to you

Someone wants to sell or rent out their property? No price, no estimate, no promise is ever written. The reply thanks them and asks the essentials — and you get a “potential listing” alert to call them yourself.

iv

Already gone? Still a lead.

If the property is under offer, the reply says so plainly, offers no viewing for it, and suggests the closest one you actually have — then asks what they are looking for.

[ FAQ ] — What agencies ask us

Honestly, before the pilot.

[ 01 ]How does it work with athome.lu inquiries?+

The portal sends you the inquiry by email. We read that message, take the property, the client’s name, email and phone, and we answer the client — not the portal’s address. Within minutes, in your agency’s name.

[ 02 ]We are rebuilding our CRM — it is not the right time.+

That is exactly why it works: we do not touch the CRM. The pilot runs on the inbox your inquiries already reach, nothing to install or migrate. Once the new CRM is ready, we look together at how to bring the conversations into it.

[ 03 ]What do I actually have to do?+

Almost nothing: add one forwarding rule on the inbox your inquiries arrive in (5 minutes, we guide you). We never get access to your inbox. Setup, tone and follow-ups are on us. In week one, you approve every reply before it goes out.

[ 04 ]What if a reply is off?+

That is what week one is for: nothing goes out without your approval. You correct, we adjust. What you approve becomes the reference.

[ 05 ]Does it replace an agent?+

No. We answer and follow up; your agents do the viewings and close. The difference: when they pick up a file, the client is already expecting their call.

[ 06 ]How much does it cost?+

The fourteen-day pilot is free. After that the price depends on the size of the agency and the volume of inquiries — leave us your email and you get a precise quote the same day.

[ 06 ] — And now

How many inquiries are waiting tonight?

One line is enough: your agency, and where your inquiries arrive. We reply the same day — and if the pilot makes no sense for you, we will say so plainly rather than sell you something.
