[ FAQ ] — The questions people ask

Honestly, before the test.

[ 01 ]How does the free test work?+

You pick one task that keeps coming back. We look at how it is done in your business today, we build the circuit on your tools, and you watch it run on real files. It is free and without commitment: at the end, you keep it or you stop.

[ 02 ]Do I have to change software?+

No. The circuit plugs into the tools you already use: your mailbox, your calendar, your files, your forms. Nothing to migrate, nothing to install on your machine.

[ 03 ]What goes out without my approval?+

Nothing, until you say so. Anything that leaves in your name follows a rule you approved, and you can put any step back under your own control.

[ 04 ]Which tasks do you take on?+

The ones that come back and always follow the same rules: quotes to prepare, appointment reminders, follow-ups, the same data entered from one tool into another, standard replies, information to gather before a meeting.

[ 05 ]How much does it cost?+

It depends on the task and on your tools: the price is set case by case, in writing, before anything starts. The test on a first real case is free — leave us your email and you get an answer the same day.

[ 06 ]What about my data?+

It stays yours: the circuit works inside your own tools, nothing is resold or reused elsewhere, and a deletion request is honoured within a day. This site has no cookies and no trackers.

[ 05 ] — And now

Tell us about the task that keeps coming back.

One line is enough: what you redo every week, and with which tools. We reply the same day — and if a circuit makes no sense for your business, we will say so plainly rather than sell you something.
