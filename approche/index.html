[ The approach ] — why I work this way

A company has a philosophy.

Before it has a website, before it has a logo. It has a reason to exist, a way of treating people, something it wants you to feel when you walk through its door. My work is to find that — and make it visible on screen.

I

[ 01 ] — Where it comes from

I come from drawing, not from code.

I went to art school: design, drawing, composition. You learn one thing there before all the others — look for a long time before you draw anything. That has stayed with me as a working method, and it is why I never start a website from a template.

A page is composed like an image: what you see first, what comes next, what is left to breathe. Rhythm, space, light. Those are a draughtsman's decisions, not technical settings — which is why two sites by the same hand do not look alike.

✎Drawing

I am very interested in psychology: why one page inspires trust and another does not, why people hesitate, why they write or why they close the tab. It is not there to manipulate anyone — it is there so nothing stands between you and the person who already wanted to talk to you.

II

[ 02 ] — What I look for in you

I do not ask which pages you want.

A brief gives a list of pages. It never says what the house is. So I ask other questions, and those are the ones that decide the site:

01

Why did you start this trade?

Not the version for the bank manager. The real one.

02

What should someone feel when they walk through your door?

Trust, calm, desire, safety, respect — it is never the same word, and everything follows from it.

03

What are you proud of that nobody notices?

That is almost always the thing to show first.

04

What are you working towards?

A site that ignores where the house is going freezes it in what it was last year.

III

Only then do I draw.

The feeling you described becomes concrete decisions — and every one of them can be explained.

IV

[ 04 ] — How we work together

Few projects, and we learn from each other.

01

We talk, at length, before I draw. In writing, at your pace, in English, French or German. You teach me your trade — its words, its seasons, what irritates you about your competitors. I cannot make visible something I have not understood.

02

You see something real very quickly. A glimpse of your world arrives in days, not months. Not a document: a page that moves, that you open on your phone. We judge the real thing — it is more honest than an illustrated quote.

03

You correct me, and you are often right. It is your house, not my gallery. When you tell me "that is not us", it is valuable information: it teaches me where your line is, and the site rings truer. I have an opinion and I defend it once — after that, you decide.

04

I stay after it goes live. A house moves: new services, new photographs, new seasons. I would rather follow a few clients for a long time than deliver many sites and forget them.
