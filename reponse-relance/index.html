[ FAQ ] — The questions people ask

Honestly, before the pilot.

[ 01 ]How does the free pilot work?+

Fourteen days, on your real inquiries, no commitment. In week one you approve every reply before it goes out. At the end you decide — and if you continue, the first month is refunded if it does not convince you.

[ 02 ]Do you really answer at night?+

Yes — evenings, weekends and public holidays included. That is the whole point: the inquiry landing at 10 pm on a Friday does not sleep until Monday.

[ 03 ]What if a reply is off?+

That is exactly why week one exists: nothing goes out without your approval. You correct the tone, we adjust. What you approve becomes the reference.

[ 04 ]In which languages?+

French, German and English — the reply goes out in the language of the message received.

[ 05 ]How much does it cost?+

The fourteen-day pilot is free. After that the price depends on your volume and your inquiry sources — leave us your email and you get a precise quote the same day.

[ 06 ]Does it affect my existing clients?+

No. We only handle new incoming inquiries. Your exchanges with current clients stay entirely yours.
